研究方向ResearchRESEARCH INTERESTS

01

纺织品洗护与护理Textile Care and Laundering

TEXTILE CARE & LAUNDERING PERFORMANCE

家庭洗涤与干衣过程中的传热传质、纤维损伤与外观保持，干衣机护理剂的施加方式。Heat and mass transfer, fibre damage and appearance retention during domestic washing and drying, and the dosing of care agents in tumble dryers.

02

染色转移机理Dye Transfer Mechanisms

DYE TRANSFER & COLOUR CROSS-STAINING

洗涤串染的传质路径、影响因素与抑制方法，异味吸附与去除。Mass-transfer pathways, influencing factors and inhibition of colour cross-staining in laundering, together with odour adsorption and removal.

03

产品碳足迹Product Carbon Footprint

CARBON FOOTPRINT & SUSTAINABILITY

服装使用阶段的温室气体核算、低碳洗护技术与可持续性评价。Greenhouse-gas accounting for the use phase of clothing, low-carbon care technologies and sustainability assessment.

04

家庭洗涤行为Domestic Laundry Behaviour

HOUSEHOLD LAUNDRY BEHAVIOUR

洗护行为的实证调研与统计建模，行为—能耗—排放的关联分析。Empirical surveys and statistical modelling of laundry behaviour, linking behaviour to energy use and emissions.

学术简介BiographyBIOGRAPHY

2018 年获东华大学服装设计与工程专业工学博士学位，同年入职上海工程技术大学纺织服装学院。研究聚焦纺织品家庭洗护过程中的传热传质、染料转移与纤维损伤机理，以及洗护行为的碳足迹核算与可持续性评价。成果发表于 Textile Research Journal、AATCC Journal of Research、International Journal of Clothing Science and Technology 及《纺织学报》等期刊；主持企业横向课题多项，参与国家自然科学基金项目。承担《数理统计方法》《服装生产管理》《服装材料学》等课程，指导硕士研究生与本科毕业设计。

He received his Ph.D. in Fashion Design and Engineering from Donghua University in 2018 and joined the School of Textiles and Fashion at Shanghai University of Engineering Science in the same year. His research centres on heat and mass transfer, dye transfer and fibre damage during domestic textile care, together with carbon-footprint accounting and sustainability assessment of laundering behaviour. His work has appeared in Textile Research Journal, AATCC Journal of Research, International Journal of Clothing Science and Technology and the Journal of Textile Research. He has led a number of industry-funded projects and has taken part in projects funded by the National Natural Science Foundation of China. He teaches Mathematical Statistics, Apparel Production Management and Clothing Materials, and supervises master’s students and undergraduate theses.
