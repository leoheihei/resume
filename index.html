研究方向ResearchRESEARCH INTERESTS

01

纺织品洗护与护理Textile Care and Laundering

TEXTILE CARE & LAUNDERING PERFORMANCE

家庭洗涤与干衣过程中的传热传质、纤维损伤与外观保持，干衣机护理剂的施加方式。Heat and mass transfer, fibre damage and appearance retention during domestic washing and drying, and the dosing of care agents in tumble dryers.

02

服装和纺织品可持续发展Sustainable Apparel and Textiles

SUSTAINABLE APPAREL & TEXTILES

服装使用阶段的温室气体核算与低碳洗护技术，洗护行为的实证调研与统计建模，行为—能耗—排放的关联分析与可持续性评价。Greenhouse-gas accounting and low-carbon care technologies for the use phase of clothing, empirical surveys and statistical modelling of laundry behaviour, and sustainability assessment linking behaviour to energy use and emissions.

学术简介BiographyBIOGRAPHY

2018 年获东华大学服装设计与工程专业工学博士学位，同年入职上海工程技术大学纺织服装学院。研究聚焦纺织品家庭洗护过程中的传热传质与纤维损伤，以及服装和纺织品的可持续发展——使用阶段的碳足迹核算、家庭洗涤行为调研与可持续性评价。成果发表于 Textile Research Journal、AATCC Journal of Research、International Journal of Clothing Science and Technology 及《纺织学报》等期刊；主持企业横向课题多项。承担《数理统计方法》《服装生产管理》《服装材料学》等课程，指导硕士研究生与本科毕业设计。

He received his Ph.D. in Fashion Design and Engineering from Donghua University in 2018 and joined the School of Textiles and Fashion at Shanghai University of Engineering Science in the same year. His research centres on heat and mass transfer and fibre damage during domestic textile care, and on the sustainability of apparel and textiles — carbon-footprint accounting for the use phase, domestic laundry behaviour, and sustainability assessment. His work has appeared in Textile Research Journal, AATCC Journal of Research, International Journal of Clothing Science and Technology and the Journal of Textile Research. He has led a number of industry-funded projects. He teaches Mathematical Statistics, Apparel Production Management and Clothing Materials, and supervises master’s students and undergraduate theses.
